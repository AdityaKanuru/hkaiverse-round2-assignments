# Net time-saved estimate (illustrative, not measured)
The assignment asks for net time saved after review/support effort. No real campus timing data was available, so this is a transparent scenario calculation.

## Assumptions per 100 queries
| Activity | Current process estimate | Proposed process estimate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Initial intake and clarification | 6 min/query | 3 min/query |
| Finding correct owner / follow-up | 7 min/query | 3 min/query |
| Review, logging and closure | 5 min/query | 4 min/query |
| Total per query | 18 min | 10 min |
| Total for 100 queries | 1,800 min | 1,000 min |

Estimated gross time saved = 1,800 − 1,000 = **800 minutes**.

## Subtract review and support
Assume an additional 15 minutes per 100 queries for monitoring routing, correcting misroutes, and maintaining the queue.

Net time saved = 800 − 15 = **785 minutes per 100 queries**, or about **13.1 hours**.

## Interpretation
This is a worked example, not a result. The result changes with query complexity, missing information, duplicate frequency, and the time spent by authorised reviewers. To validate: time a sample of queries before and during a small approved pilot; count support effort; compare like-for-like categories; report median and range, not only average.

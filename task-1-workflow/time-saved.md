# Time-Saving Estimate — Campus Loop

## Illustrative assumptions
- Requests per month: 100
- Current handling time per request: 15 minutes
- Proposed handling time per request: 8 minutes
- Estimated review and support effort: 5 hours per month

## Calculation
Current effort = 100 × 15 = 1,500 minutes = 25 hours.

Proposed effort = 100 × 8 = 800 minutes = 13.33 hours.

Gross time saved = 25 − 13.33 = 11.67 hours.

Net time saved = 11.67 − 5 = 6.67 hours per month.

## Limitation
These are illustrative estimates, not measured ICFAI data. Actual request volumes and timings must be validated.

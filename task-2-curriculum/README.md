# SkillBridge CSE — Curriculum Audit and Redesign

## Objective

Propose practical curriculum improvements for B.Tech CSE students at IFHE Hyderabad, admission batch 2023.

## Proposed Changes

* Keep essential computer science foundations.
* Update existing subjects with more practical work.
* Add AI workflow testing and an integrated portfolio capstone.
* Verify internship credits and batch-specific curriculum rules.

## Supporting Files

* `course-change-matrix.csv` — proposed course changes.
* `job-description-evidence.csv` — preliminary role-skill comparison.
* `ai-exercise-records.csv` — planned AI exercises and verification status.
* `interactive-comparison.html` — searchable curriculum comparison.

## Important Limitations

This is a student proposal, not an official ICFAI curriculum audit. The official 2023-batch curriculum, course codes, total credits, and internship credits still require verification.

The job-skill examples are preliminary and must be supported by real job-posting links. AI exercises are not complete until the outputs and human verification are recorded.
